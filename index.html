07

Key findings

What the analysis shows.

01 / Variation

Pressure is uneven.

Specialty and district comparisons reveal substantial variation in extended waits, with Orthopaedic Surgery a recurring area of pressure.

02 / Outlook

Waitlist growth remains a concern.

The central forecasts suggest a growing total waitlist while the proportion waiting over 120 days remains around the mid-30% range.

03 / Evaluation

Complexity did not improve prediction.

Random Forest and Gradient Boosting did not outperform the persistence baseline in subgroup validation.

Interpretation & limitations

The data cover June 2015–September 2025. Suppressed counts reported as <5 were estimated as 2.5. Aggregated monthly waitlist counts should not be read as unique patient counts across time. Forecasts are estimates with widening uncertainty; subgroup comparisons require care, especially for small groups.
